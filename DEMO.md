# Demo shot list — the 2-minute video

Goal: show emergence and *mechanical* gossip in one clean arc. Have the backend
+ web running, town at Day 0 morning (fresh boot). Prewarm one tick so the
embedding model is loaded (avoids a slow first tick on camera).

| # | Time | Shot | Say / show |
|---|------|------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | **Cold open on the map.** Wide shot of the village, characters idling at their homes. Press **Auto**. | "Eight AI villagers — each with a job, memories, goals, and secrets. Nobody scripted what happens next." |
| 2 | 0:12–0:30 | **Morning rush.** Characters walk out along the paths to their workplaces; the day/time badge ticks morning → afternoon. | "Every tick, one LLM call plans all eight at once — that's the trick that keeps it inside a free tier." |
| 3 | 0:30–0:48 | **Click a resident** (e.g. Silas the merchant). Inspector slides in. | "Click anyone and read their mind — current plan and *why*, their memories, how they feel about everyone else. This is real retrieved memory, not a bio." |
| 4 | 0:48–1:00 | **Evening gathering.** The whole town converges on the square; speech bubbles pop. Click a **💬 bubble** → the actual dialogue transcript. | "When they meet, they actually talk — and what they learn gets written to memory." |
| 5 | 1:00–1:15 | **Inject the event.** Town Crier bar → type/click *"a stranger arrives at the square asking about the old mine."* | "Now I drop a rumor into the square." |
| 6 | 1:15–1:45 | **Follow the hop.** Tick forward. A 🤫 ripple appears between two villagers; click it → *"X learned: …the old mine…"*. Tick again; click the next 🤫 — show the wording has **mutated**. Repeat once more. | "Watch it spread — person to person, mutating as it goes. Nobody was told to pass it on; the gossip is mechanical, because information only exists as memory." |
| 7 | 1:45–2:00 | **Pull back to the Chronicle** panel and scroll the day's narrated story; end on the latest line. | "And a narrator writes the whole thing up as a readable chronicle. Eight agents, a secret, and one afternoon — all for about seventeen LLM calls." |

**Backup beats if a set-piece doesn't fire on the day:** Bram cornering Silas
over the debt (click either → the confrontation in memory); Elara and Rurik both
turning up at Mira's café (the rivalry). These are seeded tensions, so one of
them usually surfaces within a few ticks — pick whichever fires and click into
it.

**Tips:** free-tier ticks can take 15–60s (the SSE progress label shows what
phase it's on) — cut those waits in the edit. Record at ~1440p; the canvas is
crisp (pixel-art, nearest-neighbor). Dusk (evening) is the most photogenic — glowing
windows + fireflies — so try to land the rumor-spread beats around then.
